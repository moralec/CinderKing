# Round 5: the author's ideas (23 Nov 2026)

Written down as said; each becomes a board item (issue · proposal · references) and then tested steps.

1. **Every NPC has a bit of a back story, and what they say connects to it.** That gives the game cohesiveness.
2. **Their story relates to where they are found.** For example, the lady with the rat quest talks about her cellar: she should be found there (at her house, by the cellar door), not in the middle of the map.
3. **Traders are always in a relevant place:** a travelling salesman with a cart and horses on the road, a trader in a town with a shop, a blacksmith in his workshop, and so on. The game then needs the buildings that match (shop, smithy, inn, cellars, a merchant's camp).

Related items already on the round-5 list: settlement layouts beyond one hut, placement of people and things beyond the fixed six slots (critic: "NPCs off the fixed spots"), quest-template variety and one landmark per region.

Decision (23 Nov): keep the 12 DCSS portraits whose file names are on the crawl "unclear" list; check their dates before v0.8 is published.
