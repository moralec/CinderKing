# The Cinder King: back cover

**Every night, a bell tolls beneath the grave of Brindlemere.**

Nobody in the village rang it. Nobody alive, anyway.

What begins with a restless crypt becomes a trail of ash across Aldermoor: a ledger paid in coin from a city that fell three
hundred years ago, a moon sigil scratched off a smuggler's crate, a drowned shrine whose stone whispers *"He sleeps under
Cindermaw."* And a second voice, breathless and patient, asking one question: *when will the sky go dark?*

The only blade that ever wounded the Cinder King lies in pieces. To forge it again you will cross dwarven roads, salt marshes and
the frozen north, face a choir of zealots who sing to wake a dragon, and learn that the one pulling their strings has been waiting
far longer than they have.

Some gates hold monsters out. This one holds a king in. And you will have to open it yourself.

- 36 lands to explore, from the Frostfang Shore to the Fallen Star Crater
- 52 people to meet, 8 chapters, 8 side quests woven into the main story
- 83 monsters and 87 items from the world's oldest fantasy rules (SRD 5.1)
- Classic d20 battles, a reforged sword, and an ending worth the ash

*An original adventure for one hero. Save your game; the dead do not rest.*
