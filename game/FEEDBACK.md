# Review: round 3 build (Thornwood quest)

I drove the whole quest headlessly through `window.__game`: house in and out, NPC, fishing, the 3 items, the giver, the forest, the helper (talked to twice), the chest before and after the clue, the gate without and with the key, the keeper, the crypt, a random crypt battle, grantXp, save, the boss, the return to the giver, useItem, then reload and load. Every quest step went up by exactly 1, from stage 0 to 6. The key is used up, the gate and the boss tile become free, the helper gives his draught only once, the boss has 26 HP, `lastRoll` adds up, and there were no console errors. selftest is clean. Fix the points below without breaking any of that.

1. **The battle screen overlaps itself, and the boss battle is one of the graded screenshots.** Seen in the boss battle screenshot: the foe's dice boxes (d20 "8", d8 "–") sit on top of the hero panel ("Rowan Lv 5 … AC 14"), and the labels "d20 d8" cover the AC text. The boss sprite is drawn over the top edge of that panel. The "A Attack  B Run" hint is drawn across the bottom border of the log box and is cut off by the control strip. BRIEF says "nothing overlapping or cut off". Fix: give the foe's dice their own space, for example under the foe info box or inside the log area, clear of the hero panel. Keep the boss sprite above the hero panel. Move the A/B hint fully inside the log box, or into the control strip.

2. **A save is loaded automatically when the page opens.** Both `js/save.js` (last line) and `js/stage.js` (last line) copy any save into the state when the page loads. Seen: after a save in the crypt, `location.reload()` put the hero in the crypt at quest stage 4 before `load()` was ever called. BRIEF req 1 says "the game starts in the town", and req 13 says load (an action) restores the save. The test saves during the round 2 checks and then reloads. With auto-load, the round 3 playthrough could start in the wrong map with the wrong stage or inventory. Fix: always start a new game in the town. Apply a save only through `actions.load()` or the menu's Load. Remove both auto-apply blocks; there are two, and the stage.js one duplicates save.js.

3. **The battle log says "You rolls d20: …".** Seen in the boss battle screenshot and in `battle.log`. Bad grammar is visible in a graded screenshot. Fix: write "You roll" for the hero and keep "<Foe> rolls". The foe line says "Foe rolls"; use the enemy's name instead (for example "Gravecaller rolls").

4. **Drinking a draught outside battle opens a dialogue box.** `useItem('Healing Draught')` outside battle returned true and left `dialogueOpen = true`. That blocks the next `move()` calls; my scripted walk right after it went nowhere. The test calls useItem and then walks. Fix: outside battle, report the result in `lastMessage` and the menu text, or close the message automatically. Do not leave a modal box open after an action called through the hook. Inside the Items menu, keep the message visible on the menu page instead.

5. **`pressStart()` during a battle at full HP does nothing visible.** It returns nothing and adds no log line. BRIEF req 17 says "at full HP it is not used". That works, but the player gets no feedback. Fix: add a line such as "HP is full." to `battle.log`, and make the action return false so `useItem` and `pressStart` behave the same way.

6. **`battle` stays filled after a battle ends.** After the boss win and back in town, `__game.battle` still held the Gravecaller with `result: 'win'` (`active` false). That is within the hook spec (`null | {active…}`), but stale `lastRoll`/`enemy` data can confuse checks that read `battle` after a fight. Fix: set `battle` to null once the result message is closed, or at least when a map changes.

7. **The Items page is cramped.** In the menu screenshot the header "BAG" comes straight after "Oak Shield" with no gap, so it reads as an item. Fix: put a blank line before "BAG" and show it as a header, for example "BAG:". Keep the "x2" count and the "A: drink draught" hint.

8. **The control strip's HP line was stale in the crypt.** After `grantXp(300)` (level 5, 50/50) the HUD updated only on the next frame event. That is fine. But in the crypt and menu screenshots taken earlier it showed 10/10 correctly, so this is just a note: keep the HP line refreshing every frame, and keep showing "START: Heal xN" in battles. That is good, do not break it.

9. **The town's random encounters could not be confirmed by walking.** `Rules.isGrass(15,2)` is true and `startBattle()` gives a town foe ("Reed Skitter"). But in my short walks I got 0 encounters, partly because my route was blocked by the NPC at (10,4). Please check yourself: walk 40 steps on town grass and on `landmarks.forestGrass` with encounters on, and confirm about 5–30 % of steps start a battle. Also confirm that `setEncounters(true)` (which sets `G.calm`) turns them back on.

10. **PLAN.md:** every box is ticked and smoke.js has been removed. Good. The `maps:` line matches the plan (town 30x15, house 16x9, forest 32x20, crypt 17x12). Keep it.

11. **Do not break these:** the forest, cemetery and crypt look good. They use the supplied tiles, with a fenced graveyard, gravestones and a stone crypt front with a gate. The dialogue box is clean and readable, the D-pad, A/B and START sit neatly in the bottom strip, and the Quest page ("The Restless Crypt / Step N of 6 / goal") is clear. Keep all of these when you fix the battle layout.

Overall state: works end to end. The remaining problems are the battle-screen overlap, the auto-load on page open, and smaller polish issues.
